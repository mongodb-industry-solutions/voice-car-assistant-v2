# 1. Vehicle Identity and Purpose

## Brand and Model

- Brand: MongoDB
- Model: Leafy 1.0

## Vehicle Character

The MongoDB Leafy 1.0 is a fictional compact plug-in hybrid hatchback intended for daily road use. It combines:

- front-wheel drive
- a gasoline engine
- an electric traction motor
- a floor-mounted high-voltage battery
- a fuel tank and fuel delivery system
- regenerative braking
- a five-door body configuration
- seating for five occupants
- a digital driver information system
- a practical rear cargo compartment

The Leafy 1.0 is intended for paved-road driving. It is not designed as an off-road vehicle, a race vehicle, or a towing-heavy utility vehicle.

### Front exterior troubleshooting

#### Problem: hood will not open

Possible causes:

- interior hood release not fully pulled
- secondary latch stuck
- hood slightly loaded by pressure or dirt
- latch mechanism dry or contaminated

What to do:

1. Pull the interior hood release fully.
2. Press down lightly on the hood, then lift again.
3. Reach under the front edge and move the secondary latch fully aside.
4. If still stuck, do not force the hood upward from one corner.

#### Problem: front parking sensor warning appears with no obstacle

Possible causes:

- dirt, mud, snow, or water film on sensor face
- heavy rain
- direct reflective surface nearby
- sensor misalignment

What to do:

1. Clean the sensor area gently.
2. Recheck after restarting the vehicle.
3. If warning persists in clear conditions, service inspection is recommended.

#### Problem: charge port door does not open

Possible causes:

- vehicle locked
- actuator delay
- ice around charge door
- debris in door seam

What to do:

1. Unlock the vehicle fully.
2. Press the charge port door again.
3. If frozen, warm the area gently. Do not pry with sharp tools.
4. If repeated failure occurs, use service support.

# 7. Powertrain and Hybrid Systems

## Main drive system

The MongoDB Leafy 1.0 uses a plug-in hybrid propulsion system made up of:

- gasoline engine
- fuel tank and fuel delivery system
- high-voltage traction battery
- battery management system
- inverter
- electric traction motor
- hybrid drive unit
- regenerative braking system
- DC-DC converter
- low-voltage support battery
- cooling pumps
- thermal management valves
- high-voltage contactors
- orange high-voltage cables

## How the system works

When the vehicle is turned on and shows Ready mode, the Leafy 1.0 can move using the electric motor, the gasoline engine, or both together depending on speed, battery charge, temperature, and power demand. During gentle driving or short-distance operation, the vehicle may favor electric propulsion. During stronger acceleration, higher speeds, steep climbs, or low battery conditions, the gasoline engine may start automatically to assist propulsion or maintain battery charge. When slowing down, regenerative braking allows part of the vehicle’s motion energy to be returned to the battery.

## Normal hybrid vehicle behaviors

The following can be normal:

- a click sound at startup or shutdown
- the gasoline engine starting automatically
- cooling fan noise after parking
- pump sounds while charging
- changes between electric and engine operation
- reduced power when battery is very cold or very hot
- stronger deceleration in high regeneration mode

### Powertrain troubleshooting

#### Problem: vehicle powers on but does not move

Possible causes:

- Ready mode not active
- gear not fully selected
- parking brake still engaged
- system fault limiting drive

What to do:

1. Confirm Ready indicator is shown.
2. Press brake and select D or R again.
3. Check for warning messages.
4. If a red drive system warning appears, do not continue driving.

#### Problem: acceleration feels weaker than normal

Possible causes:

- low battery charge
- battery too cold or too hot
- Eco or Sprout mode selected
- traction control limiting wheel slip
- power-limiting warning active

What to do:

1. Check battery state of charge.
2. Review drive mode selection.
3. Allow battery conditioning if temperatures are extreme.
4. If power remains limited in mild conditions, inspect warning messages.

#### Problem: whining sound during acceleration

Possible causes:

- normal electric motor tone
- inverter switching sound
- reduction gear sound

What to do:

A light smooth electric whine is often normal. A harsh grinding, knocking, or sudden change in pitch is not normal and should be inspected.

# 8. Battery, Fuel, and Charging

## Hybrid energy overview

The Leafy 1.0 stores energy in two ways: gasoline in the fuel tank and electrical energy in the high-voltage battery. The hybrid control system automatically decides when to use the engine, when to use the electric motor, and when to combine both. Drivers may notice the gasoline engine starting and stopping automatically depending on battery charge, outside temperature, cabin heating or cooling demand, and driving load.

## High-voltage battery

The floor-mounted traction battery contains:

- battery modules
- cell monitoring electronics
- coolant channels
- structural tray
- protective enclosure
- service disconnect access point
- impact protection structure

Do not puncture, modify, heat, flood, open, or attempt to repair the battery pack.

## 12V support battery

The vehicle also uses a low-voltage support battery to power computers, locks, lights, and control systems. If this battery is weak, the vehicle may show unusual electrical behavior even when the main traction battery has charge.

## Charging system

Charging components include:

- charge port
- charge port lock
- AC charging interface
- DC fast-charge interface
- onboard charger
- charging communication unit
- charging cable recognition system
- charge ring indicator

## Charging light meanings

- white pulsing: initializing
- green pulsing: charging
- green solid: charging complete
- amber: charging paused or limited
- red flashing: charging fault

## Charging guidance

Before charging:

- park safely
- shift to Park
- inspect cable and connector
- keep the port clean and dry

During charging:

- avoid strain on the connector
- do not force locked connectors
- follow station instructions

### Battery and charging troubleshooting

#### Problem: charging does not start

Possible causes:

- connector not fully inserted
- charging station issue
- schedule set for later
- charge port lock not engaged
- battery temperature outside ideal range

What to do:

1. Disconnect and reconnect the charger fully.
2. Verify the station is active.
3. Check in-screen charging schedule settings.
4. Watch the charge light ring.
5. Try another charging source if available.

#### Problem: charging starts then stops

Possible causes:

- station communication failure
- power interruption
- thermal limit
- cable strain
- battery protection event

What to do:

1. Inspect connector seating.
2. Restart the charging session.
3. If on DC charging, try AC charging as a comparison.
4. Repeated red fault indications require service inspection.

#### Problem: connector will not release

Possible causes:

- vehicle still locked to port
- charging session still active
- actuator delay
- cold-weather freezing around connector

What to do:

1. Stop charging from the station first.
2. Unlock the vehicle.
3. Wait a few seconds and try again.
4. If icy, warm the area gently.

#### Problem: range is lower than expected after full charge

Possible causes:

- cold weather
- recent high-speed driving
- heavy climate use
- tire pressure low
- route elevation
- battery estimation recalculating

What to do:

1. Review recent driving conditions.
2. Check tire pressures.
3. Precondition cabin while plugged in.
4. Use Eco or Sprout mode when needed.

### Steering and suspension troubleshooting

#### Problem: steering feels heavier than normal

Possible causes:

- low assist mode due to fault
- low 12V support battery
- steering module warning
- tire pressure low

What to do:

1. Check for steering warning light.
2. Check tire pressures.
3. Restart the vehicle safely.
4. Persistent heavy steering requires service.

#### Problem: vehicle pulls to one side

Possible causes:

- uneven tire pressure
- road crown effect
- wheel alignment issue
- brake drag
- tire construction variation

What to do:

1. Check and equalize tire pressures.
2. Test again on a flatter road.
3. If pull remains, alignment or brake inspection may be needed.

#### Problem: clunk over bumps

Possible causes:

- suspension bushing wear
- loose cargo creating false noise
- top mount wear
- stabilizer link movement

What to do:

1. Remove loose cargo first.
2. If noise remains over repeated bumps, inspect suspension hardware.

### Tire troubleshooting

#### Problem: tire pressure warning on display

Possible causes:

- one or more tires below target pressure
- sudden temperature drop
- puncture
- sensor learning delay after tire service

What to do:

1. Inspect all tires visually.
2. Inflate to the recommended pressure.
3. If one tire drops again quickly, treat as a leak.

#### Problem: uneven tire wear

Possible causes:

- underinflation
- overinflation
- alignment issue
- suspension wear
- infrequent tire rotation

What to do:

1. Inspect tread across inside, center, and outside.
2. Correct pressures.
3. Rotate or align as needed.

## Normal brake behavior

Some mild sounds can be normal, especially after moisture, overnight parking, or self-check events.

### Brake troubleshooting

#### Problem: brake pedal feels firmer than usual

Possible causes:

- cold-weather condition
- brake assist self-check
- temporary vacuum or boost characteristic
- hydraulic issue

What to do:

1. Start the vehicle fully in Ready mode.
2. Apply pedal several times while stationary.
3. If a brake warning is shown, do not continue driving.

#### Problem: squeal from brakes

Possible causes:

- moisture film on rotors
- light corrosion after parking
- pad wear
- debris between pad and rotor

What to do:

1. Drive carefully and brake gently a few times.
2. If squeal continues when dry and warm, inspect pads.

#### Problem: grinding noise when braking

Possible causes:

- severe pad wear
- trapped debris
- rotor damage

What to do:

Do not ignore grinding. Brake components may be damaged and should be inspected promptly.

#### Problem: parking brake will not release

Possible causes:

- brake not commanded off
- low 12V support battery
- system fault
- frozen rear brake components in cold weather

What to do:

1. Press brake pedal firmly.
2. Command release again.
3. Start vehicle fully in Ready mode.
4. If still locked, do not force driving.

# 17A. Flat Tire Repair, Temporary Repair, and Wheel Change

## Tire puncture guidance

The MongoDB Leafy 1.0 may be equipped with a tire mobility kit instead of a full-size spare wheel. In this configuration, small tread-area punctures may sometimes be repaired temporarily with sealant and an air compressor. Sidewall damage, torn tires, damaged wheels, blowouts, or tires that have been driven flat for too long should not be repaired using a mobility kit.

## If a flat tire occurs while driving

If you suspect a flat tire, reduce speed gradually and avoid sudden steering inputs. Hold the steering wheel firmly, move the vehicle to a safe location away from traffic, and stop on level ground if possible.

Steps:

1. Slow down gradually.
2. Turn on the hazard flashers.
3. Move to a safe shoulder or parking area.
4. Shift to Park.
5. Apply the parking brake.
6. Exit only when safe.
7. Place a warning triangle if available and appropriate.

## Inspecting the tire

Before attempting any repair, inspect the tire carefully.

A temporary mobility-kit repair may be possible if:

- the puncture is in the tread area
- the damage is small
- the tire is still seated on the wheel
- the wheel itself is not bent or cracked

Do not attempt temporary repair if:

- the sidewall is cut, split, or bulging
- the wheel is damaged
- the tire has come off the rim
- the puncture is large
- multiple areas are damaged
- the vehicle was driven extensively on a fully flat tire

## Using the tire mobility kit

The mobility kit usually includes:

- sealant bottle
- air hose
- power cable
- pressure gauge or digital pressure display
- selector for air or sealant mode

General procedure:

1. Park safely and secure the vehicle.
2. Remove the mobility kit from the cargo area.
3. Read the kit markings and connect the hose to the tire valve.
4. Connect the power cable to the vehicle accessory socket if applicable.
5. Inject sealant according to kit directions.
6. Inflate the tire to the target emergency pressure shown on the kit or tire guide.
7. Drive slowly for a short distance so the sealant can spread inside the tire.
8. Stop and recheck pressure.
9. If pressure will not hold, do not continue driving.

After temporary repair:

- drive only at reduced speed
- avoid long distances
- have the tire professionally inspected or replaced as soon as possible

## How to change a wheel if a spare wheel is provided

If the vehicle version includes a spare wheel and tools, the wheel may be changed manually. Always work on level ground.

Required items:

- jack
- wheel wrench
- spare wheel
- warning triangle
- gloves if available

Wheel-change procedure:

1. Move the vehicle to a safe level location.
2. Turn on hazard flashers.
3. Shift to Park.
4. Apply the parking brake.
5. Place wheel chocks or secure the vehicle from rolling if available.
6. Remove the wheel cover if fitted.
7. Loosen the wheel nuts slightly before lifting the vehicle.
8. Position the jack only at the approved jacking point.
9. Raise the vehicle until the flat tire clears the ground.
10. Remove the wheel nuts fully.
11. Remove the flat tire.
12. Mount the spare wheel onto the hub.
13. Install the wheel nuts finger-tight first.
14. Lower the vehicle carefully.
15. Tighten the wheel nuts in a crisscross pattern.
16. Stow the damaged wheel and tools securely.
17. Check tire pressure as soon as possible.

## Important warnings during tire change

- Never place any part of the body under a vehicle supported only by a jack.
- Never jack the vehicle on soft, uneven, or sloped ground.
- Never start the vehicle while it is jacked up.
- Never use damaged tools.
- Never loosen or remove fasteners other than the wheel nuts for the affected wheel.
- Never attempt wheel change near moving traffic if the area is unsafe.

## After installing a spare wheel

If the spare is temporary or space-saving:

- drive at reduced speed
- avoid hard braking and sharp cornering
- repair or replace the original tire as soon as possible
- do not use the temporary spare for long-distance driving unless specifically intended for it

## Flat tire troubleshooting

### Problem: compressor runs but tire does not inflate

Possible causes:

- puncture too large
- hose not seated properly
- valve damaged
- tire bead unseated from rim
- compressor overheating or faulty

What to do:

1. Stop the attempt.
2. Check all hose connections.
3. Inspect for severe tire or wheel damage.
4. If tire will not take air, do not drive the vehicle.

### Problem: tire inflates but pressure drops again quickly

Possible causes:

- sealant unable to close puncture
- sidewall damage
- valve leak
- wheel rim damage

What to do:

The repair should be treated as unsuccessful. Do not continue except for the minimum needed to reach immediate safety if absolutely necessary.

### Problem: jack feels unstable

Possible causes:

- incorrect jacking point
- uneven ground
- jack not centered
- vehicle shifting during lift

What to do:

Lower the vehicle immediately and reposition the jack on stable, level ground.

# 17C. Fuel-Related Problems and Solutions

## Important note about fuel in the Leafy 1.0

The MongoDB Leafy 1.0 is a fictional plug-in hybrid vehicle. It uses both gasoline and stored electrical energy. This means the vehicle has a real fuel system as well as a hybrid battery and charging system.

Main fuel-related components include:

- fuel filler door
- fuel filler neck
- fuel tank
- fuel pump
- fuel lines
- fuel injectors
- gasoline engine
- evaporative emissions components
- fuel-level sender
- fuel gauge

## Common fuel-related concerns

### Problem: low fuel warning appears

Possible causes:

- fuel tank level is low
- recent driving consumed more fuel than expected
- the vehicle has operated in engine-priority conditions for an extended period

Possible solutions:

1. Refuel with the correct gasoline grade for the vehicle.
2. Avoid running the tank extremely low.
3. Monitor whether the gauge and warning clear after refueling.

### Problem: fuel gauge drops faster than expected

Possible causes:

- aggressive driving
- high-speed cruising
- cold weather
- long climbs
- frequent short trips with repeated engine warm-up cycles
- low tire pressure
- heavy cargo load
- hybrid battery state low, causing more engine use

Possible solutions:

1. Check tire pressures.
2. Drive more smoothly.
3. Reduce unnecessary cargo.
4. Use Eco mode when appropriate.
5. Keep the hybrid battery charged when possible on plug-in versions.

### Problem: driver cannot open fuel door

Possible causes:

- vehicle locked
- fuel-door release delay
- ice or debris around fuel door
- latch issue

Possible solutions:

1. Unlock the vehicle fully.
2. Press the fuel door again or use the cabin release if equipped.
3. If frozen, warm the area gently.
4. Do not pry the door sharply with hard tools.

### Problem: vehicle was fueled but still shows low-fuel warning

Possible causes:

- fuel gauge update delay
- sensor issue
- insufficient fuel added to clear reserve threshold

Possible solutions:

1. Drive for a short distance and recheck.
2. Restart the vehicle.
3. If the warning remains after a normal refuel, inspect the fuel-level sensor system.

### Problem: engine runs more often than expected after refueling

Possible causes:

- low hybrid battery charge
- cold engine warm-up cycle
- cabin heating demand
- defrost operation
- steep terrain or high-speed driving

Possible solutions:

1. Check hybrid battery charge level.
2. Drive in a smoother, lower-demand style.
3. Charge the traction battery if plug-in charging is available.
4. Reduce unnecessary climate load when practical.

### Problem: fuel smell around vehicle

Possible causes:

- loose fuel cap if fitted
- spill during refueling
- evaporative system leak
- fuel-line or tank issue

Possible solutions:

1. Confirm the filler cap or capless filler area is properly closed.
2. Clean any spilled fuel from the exterior.
3. If odor persists strongly, have the fuel system inspected promptly.

### Problem: wrong fuel added

Possible causes:

- incorrect gasoline grade
- accidental contamination
- foreign liquid introduced during refueling

Possible solutions:

1. Do not continue driving if contamination is suspected.
2. Arrange inspection and fuel-system service.
3. Repeated rough running after refueling should not be ignored.
